## Per-pair integrity: clean

Every committed binary pair in both runs carries a yes/no answer at confidence
>= 0.65 with a source URL and an evidence quote. Every abstention is a real
`inconclusive`. The pairs whose terminal status reads `accepted_*` while the
answer is `inconclusive` at 0.62 are the D37 floor working as designed: the
adjudicator reached a sub-floor answer and the coordinator suppressed it to an
abstention. The one non-binary committed answer (PT34:NL, "yes, or all public
sector data providers already publish data") is the verbatim ODMI gold option
for that question, matched exactly in both runs.

## Reading (provisional)

The direction matches the pre-registration on every axis. The commit/abstain
decision is the unstable part (0.82, right at the M1 bar), and the disagreements
are threshold crossings between commit and no-commit rather than label flips.
Once a pair commits, the label is stable (0.95). And 68% of the pairs where both
runs commit and agree reached that answer through a **different** source URL,
which is the direct evidence for the S2.2 claim that agreement across
independent evidence paths is worth more than one computation repeated.

The two runs also give S4.2 an empirical noise floor: run-to-run commit accuracy
moves 0.009 (0.691 vs 0.682), against the 0.649-0.726 span of the ablation
ladder. The ladder's steps are larger than the run-to-run noise.

**None of this is final.** Three-run unanimity will be lower than these pairwise
figures, the Fleiss kappa intervals need all three runs, and this is the burned
dev battery. The numbers are recorded here so the direction is on the record
before rep3 lands, not to stand as a result.
